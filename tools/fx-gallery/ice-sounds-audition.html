Thin ice: creak and break sounds

16 new CC0 candidates, trimmed and loudness-matched to within 0.4 dB of each other, plus the bundled creak set. Nothing is in assets/ and nothing ships until you pick. Press play, mark each file keep or reject, then copy the line at the bottom and paste it back. An amber edge means the measurement flagged it. Full tables: ice-break-candidates/README.md.

Pair a break may be two sounds

Three shapes are possible: one ice-into-water file alone, a creak then a splash, or an ice file with more water behind it. Pick two, set the gap, and hear them as one event.
